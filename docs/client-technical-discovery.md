# Client Technical Discovery Questionnaire

For Cristian to use during the first technical/client meeting. Not every question applies to every client - skip what's irrelevant, but don't skip a section without confirming it's genuinely not applicable.

## Business

- What support do you currently provide, and how?
- What channels do your customers use today?
- What countries do you operate in?
- What languages are required?
- What are your operating hours (and do you need coverage outside them)?
- What message/conversation volume do you expect (daily/weekly)?
- What response-time expectations do you have today, and what are you hoping for?

## Channels

- Website chat?
- Email?
- WhatsApp?
- Instagram?
- Messenger?
- Other channels? (SMS, phone, marketplace messaging, etc.)

## Systems

- Shopify?
- WooCommerce?
- CRM (which one)?
- Helpdesk (which one)?
- ERP?
- Custom API?
- Other systems operators would need to reference?

## Operations

- How many operators do you expect to need?
- What's your escalation process today - who gets pulled in, and when?
- Do you need a named supervisor/point of contact on your side?
- What reporting do you need, and how often?
- What SLA do you expect (response time, resolution time)? - captured here, formally agreed later.

## Data

- What customer information will operators need access to?
- What order information will operators need access to?
- How should operators authenticate a customer's identity before discussing account details?
- Any privacy/compliance requirements (GDPR, industry-specific regulation)?
- What data retention period do you require?

## Integration

- Do you have API documentation for the systems above?
- Do you have webhook documentation?
- What credentials/access will you provide, and through what secure channel? (Never share credentials over chat/Telegram - see `docs/client-onboarding-checklist.md`.)
- Do you have a sandbox/test environment we can integrate against first?
- What rate limits apply to your APIs?
- What internal approvals do you need before granting API access (e.g. a security review on your side)?

## Pilot

- What exact scope should the 7-day pilot cover?
- What start date works?
- What does success look like, concretely?
- Which channels are included in the pilot?
- How many operators should be involved?
- What reporting do you want to see during and after the pilot?
