## 4. Recommended design
- **One new record for every public submission** (`InboxItem`): category, source form, language, received time, status, payload (the fields), plus an **append-only history** table (status changes, notes, replies logged, who and when). **Client inquiries and pilot requests also create or update a CRM lead**, linked to the inbox item, so the existing sales pipeline stages keep working. Existing careers rows and the three leads are migrated.
- **Notification service:** every new item creates an in-app alert for the Founder and an **email to founder@** with: category, date and time, the sender's name, and a link that opens the item **after sign-in**. **The message text, email address, phone and other fields are not in the email.** If the mail send fails, the item is flagged "not notified" and retried, and the Founder dashboard shows a visible count of unnotified items, so a failed email is never silent.
- **Reply:** a "Reply" button opens the Founder's own mail program (Gmail) with the address and a subject filled in, and a one-click "log that I replied" so the history is kept. Sending from inside the Workspace is a later step (it needs a mail integration and its own review).
- **Access:** Founder only (`CEO_ADMIN`); the CTO, managers and operators cannot read applicants; each view of an item is audited; no personal data in application logs.
- **Spam and abuse:** keep honeypot, rate limit and size limit; add a minimum fill-time check and duplicate throttling; consider Cloudflare Turnstile (free; a third party that must be named in the privacy notice; needs a Cloudflare setting change, so a separate approval).
- **Two paths:** `/careers` becomes "Work with GCO" (operators and moderators) and a new `/partners` page with its own form for sales partners and business partners; the existing `/contact` stays for clients and gets a "which service?" field, estimated volume and desired start date.
