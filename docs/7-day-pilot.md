# GCO 7-Day Free Pilot

The standard offer for a new prospective client after a positive discovery call: a scope-defined, monitored, 7-day live operational pilot - not an open-ended trial and not unlimited custom development.

## What the pilot is

- **Scope-defined in advance.** Before Day 1, we agree exactly what channel(s), volume, and hours the pilot covers.
- **Technical feasibility confirmed beforehand.** Any integration the pilot needs (see `docs/client-capability-matrix.md`) is confirmed buildable and, where it needs the client's API access, that access is arranged before the pilot starts - not discovered mid-pilot.
- **Client access provided beforehand.** Whatever the client needs to give us (API keys, a test/sandbox channel, etc.) happens before Day 1.
- **A controlled, monitored operation.** Real operators, real supervision, for the agreed scope only.
- **Reviewed at the end**, with a clear go/no-go conversation on continuing.

## What the pilot is not

- Not unlimited custom development during the 7 days - new integrations or features outside the agreed scope are scoped as follow-on work, not squeezed into the pilot window.
- Not a substitute for the discovery call - a pilot only starts once scope and access are settled.

## Timeline

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Before Day 1** | Discovery call, technical feasibility assessment, client access/credentials arranged. |
| **Day 1** | Configuration, integration testing, operator onboarding for this specific client's workflow/tone. |
| **Days 2-6** | Live pilot operation - real conversations, real operators, real supervision, within the agreed scope. |
| **Day 7** | Results review and continuation discussion. |
